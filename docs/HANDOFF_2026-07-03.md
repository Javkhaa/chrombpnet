## Next options (pick a direction — the two model-based ideas are exhausted)
1. **Accept the honest scope**: healthy composition works (validated); cancer/minor-fraction
   detection needs more signal. Levers: **genome-wide markers** (chr1/3/6 -> all chroms, more
   sites -> better sensitivity — biggest cheap lever, needs rebuilding A over all chroms),
   higher-ctDNA samples, or a fragment-length (DELFI) feature block.
2. **Try the model's PROFILE head** (untried) as the prediction target instead of counts —
   might carry cell-type-specific spatial info the count head lacks (uncertain; the conditioning
   limitation may persist).
3. **Fix the model's spatial conditioning** — bigger effort; the FiLM conditioning captures
   magnitude not spatial pattern. Would need architecture/training changes.
4. **Step back / write up** — the healthy-composition validation + the honest negatives
   (nucleosome, Role-b, cancer detection) are a coherent story worth documenting as-is.

Recommended: **option 1 (genome-wide markers)** — it's the one untried cheap lever that could
move minor-fraction sensitivity, and it doesn't depend on the model.
